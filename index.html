About Me

Hi! I'm Roshni, an energy and environmental policy professional passionate about creating data-driven solutions for our planet's most pressing challenges.

I hold a Master of Public Policy from the University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy and a BS in Environmental Science from the University of California, Riverside, College of Natural & Agricultural Sciences. My work focuses on the intersection of environmental policy, energy systems, and data analytics, translating complex data into actionable insights that inform better policy decisions, advance climate action, and create actionable recommendations

Outside of work, I enjoy running, baking, and anything crafty! I believe in a future where sustainable energy is accessible to all, and I'm committed to being part of that transformation.
